📞 Call Center Performance Analysis
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes 28,000+ customer interactions from a call center to identify operational inefficiencies, customer satisfaction drivers, workforce utilization gaps, and revenue opportunities.

The objective was to transform raw operational data into actionable business insights and recommend strategic initiatives, including a ₹1 crore investment plan.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze operational efficiency
Understand customer satisfaction drivers
Evaluate workforce utilization
Identify revenue optimization opportunities
Build an interactive dashboard
Recommend data-driven business strategies

🛠 Tools Used
Microsoft Excel
Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
Advanced Excel Functions
Slicers & Interactive Dashboard
Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering

📊 Key Analysis
Operational Efficiency
Customer Satisfaction
Revenue Analysis
Agent Performance
Workload Distribution
Customer Segmentation

🔍 Key Insights
Only ~40% of calls were successfully completed.
~20% calls resulted in No Answer.
Chat channel handled the highest volume but had the lowest customer ratings.
Around 65% of agents were underutilized.
Revenue was highly concentrated in call consultations.

💡 Recommendations
Intelligent call routing
AI-assisted chat support
Workforce optimization
Customer experience improvements
Performance-based agent allocation
Revenue diversification strategy
